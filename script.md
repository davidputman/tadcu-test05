# Tadcu — Episode 5: "The Fair"

*Series: Tadcu · Episode 5 · Production: test05*

---

## Cold open — TITLE CARD (0:00–0:05.5)

Sky at show-day blue. Bunting in the title. Somewhere, a ewe stretches.

> **TADCU**
> *in "The Fair"*
>
> Subtitle: *Y Sioe!*

### SCENE 1 — THE QUEUE (0:05.5–0:19.5)

**EXT. THE COUNTY SHOW GATES — MORNING**

Two stalls, one banner (SIOE SIR — hand-painted, an A upside down), one
queue. Tadcu, Bryn, and — increíble — four ewes, standing in line like
members.

- **NARRATOR:** "Cwm Twp shows up for the county show exactly as it shows up
  for weather: late, in layers, all of it."
- **TADCU:** "Tickets, Bryn. Count the girls. Don't count Dott — she came
  as COMPETITION."
- **NARRATOR:** "The queue moved at the speed of civic pie."
- **BRYN:** *(ticket in mouth. professional.)*

### SCENE 2 — THE RING (0:19.5–0:34.5)

**INT./EXT. THE SHOWING RING**

Nerys judges from the middle (her second county job — everyone local has
two), scribbling on the same clipboard that signed the vet's ledger.

- **NARRATOR:** "Into the ring walked one extremely punctual ewe."
- **NERYS:** "Movement: good. Spots: generous. Attitude: Welsh."
- **TADCU:** "(from the fence) That's OUR ewe, that is. Valued at two
  fields and a gate."
- **NARRATOR:** "Dott stopped, mid-ring, and chewed."
- **BRYN:** *(chews. wins.)*

### SCENE 3 — THE PRIZE (0:34.5–0:47.5)

**EXT. THE TEA STALL — IMMEDIATELY AFTER**

Dilys runs the tea stall (her third). Dott wears a rosette the size of a
dinner plate. Tadcu, in his best cardigan, receives strangers' nods.

- **TADCU:** "First rosette in the family since the butters, 1989."
- **DILYS:** "Sugar's free, Tadcu. Nobody can take the week away, mind."
- **NARRATOR:** "And the rosette — one red circle, scaled to ambition —
  went straight on the ewe."

### SCENE 4 — THE HOMECOMING (0:47.5–1:01.5)

**EXT. THE ROAD HOME — EVENING**

Dots of rosette-red bob down the lane. Bryn in the bandana. Sheep cheer
on both banks, hooves inappropriate for clapping.

- **NARRATOR:** "And so, home, up the lane — where the gate waited,
  wide open, like a dog who knew this was coming."
- **TADCU:** "Nos da, chi gyd. Rosettes for nobody: we were ALL there —
  and Dott did it PERSONALLY."
- **THE GIRLS:** *(the applause, one field wide)*

**THE END.**

> *Next episode: Tadcu invests in a latch. Bryn invests in a dream.
