# Tadcu — Episode 5: "The Fair"

An episode of the **Tadcu** animated cartoon series. Tadcu (Welsh for
"Grandpa"), his sheepdog Bryn, one competitive spotty ewe, and the county
show — where everyone has two jobs and the tea is a spectator sport.

## What's in here

## How to watch

Open `index.html` in a browser, press ▶ (or space). Controls: play/pause,
restart, scene skip, click the bar to scrub by scene, keys 1–6 to jump to a
scene.

## Episode 5 summary

Fair day. The gate opens like a good idea, the queue moves at the speed of
civic pie, and Dott — the spotty ewe, this series' only true professional —
walks into the showing ring, stops dead, chews, and wins. Nerys judges
(her second county job), Dilys pours at the tea stall (her third), and the
homecoming is loud.

## New in this episode

- **The fair** — bunting, a rosette, a tea stall, and one suspect plaque.
- **The judge's eye** — held by Nerys, whose verdicts have been proven on
  parcels, bills and the odd raffle.
- **Dott's career** — now technically a contestant.
